Your privacy on this site

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tags. It remembers your choice of light or dark theme in your own browser, and nowhere else.

The typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which receives your browser's address when the page loads.

The contact form is not connected yet: nothing you type is sent. When it is, this page will say who receives your request, why, and for how long it is kept.

The privacy of guest data inside the Ellie application is covered on the privacy and security page.
